Soglia

Book and Algorithmic research

A research project exploring the minimal conditions required for an image to remain perceivable and recognizable. Using an algorithmic process that progressively reduces intermediate luminance values across various photographs, the project investigates the exact threshold where visual information collapses and an image becomes unrecognizable.

The result is a publication of selected images that tests the stability and limits of perception, identifying the last point in which they remain recognizable
