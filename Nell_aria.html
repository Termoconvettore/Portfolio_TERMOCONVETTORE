Nell'aria

Visual Identity & Workshop

In collaboration with Alice Burli and Anna Benazzoli from OHT, we led a workshop on activating public spaces, using the On Festival as a case study. Afterward, we created a visual restitution of the workshop's outcomes, translating participants' ideas into a tangible object: a custom matchbox symbolizing the spark needed to ignite dialogue and community engagement.

Thx to Trento Capitale Europea del Volontariato 2024 @OHT, @Nell'aria, @Alice Burli,
