On festival

Visual Identity & Festival Organization

On festival is a youth‑driven cultural festival created to offer a space for conversations that are often overlooked in traditional educational settings. Realized in 2024 by a collective of students from high school and university, in collaboration with the Mart of Rovereto and the OHT company (Office for a Human Theatre) as part of the European project The Floor Is Yours, I contributed to the ideation of the festival, to the organizations of workshops and to the creation of the visual identity of the festival. The visual identity, encompassing posters, flyers, T-shirts, and Instagram posts and stories, was entirely co-designed with Alice Burli. "On" represents ignition and openness; it is a free space where everyone is welcome. The visual language captures the childlike wonder of the festival, its freedom, and its vibrancy.

Collaborators: @Alice Burli, @OHT, @Mart, and fellow students: Alessandro Barzan, Anna Benazzoli, Annalisa Casagranda, Camila Gadler, Chiara Mottes, Elisa Pezza, Emma Leoni, Filippo Andreatta, Francesco Margoni, Irene Matassoni, Margherita Perottoni, Michela Huang, Niccolò Simonetti, and Valeria Fumanelli.

Guests: Marina Misaghi, Giulia Marsala, Ilaria Gasperotti, Francesca De Pretis, Irene Manfrini, Sofia Pasotto, Simone Rastelli (Juta), Cello Girls, ARCI, Valerio Lampis, and DJ Kandeesha.
