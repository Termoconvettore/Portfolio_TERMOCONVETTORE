Little Fun Zine

Workshop

A collaborative workshop directed with Alice Burli within the framework of Palace in a Landscape – OHT open studio. The workshop used the fanzine as a medium to inhabit space and foster relational practices. After a brainstorming session where participants co-created prompts and activities, followed by a quick book-binding tutorial, attendees began crafting their own publications. Each page featured an interactive prompt designed to spark conversation and connection.
