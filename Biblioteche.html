Specialmente in Biblioteca

Modular Poster Design

Proposal for the project “Specialmente in Biblioteca,” conceived by the Istituzione Gian Franco Minguzzi within the framework of “Formiche Verdi” to promote the network of specialized libraries in Bologna.

The concept centers on the copy “It’s not just about books… it’s about community,” highlighting the broader value these specialized spaces offer. The campaign includes a general poster alongside specific iterations tailored for individual libraries. Each version pairs the primary format of materials held in that library with a core community value: for example, “It’s not just about magazines, but about confrontation.”

Each poster presents a book in a different position filled with numerous post-it notes, representing human presence in the books, lives and communities that entwine in the different libraries.
