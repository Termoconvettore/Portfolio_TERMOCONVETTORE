Liminal Spaces as a Depiction of Love

Conceptual and photographic research

A conceptual and photographic exploration of emotional and physical liminal spaces. This small publication examines how the contemporary concept of liminal spaces is directly linked to a modern, detached shade of romantic love, pairing theoretical text with a series of original photographs.
