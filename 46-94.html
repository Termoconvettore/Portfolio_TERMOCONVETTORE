Quarantasei
Novantaquattro

Visual Identity & Exhibition Concept

With Diego Piccioni, Federico Veronica and Enrico Manunza I co-created the concept and visual identity for an hypothetical exhibition about the Italian Prima Repubblica. The exposition works on the posters of the archive manifestipolitici.it by the Fondazione Gramsci Emilia-Romagna.

Our design concept bridges the physical nature of the poster with the passage of time. The exhibition’s logo is made out of a folded poster, symbolizing both the primary medium of the show and a timeline spanning the years of the Prima Repubblica, which also inspires the title: '46-'94, Quarantasei-Novantaquattro.

The entire visual identity was developed by: @Diego Piccioni, @Federico Veronica, @Valeria Fumanelli and @Enrico Manunza with the supervision of Sara Ceradini.
