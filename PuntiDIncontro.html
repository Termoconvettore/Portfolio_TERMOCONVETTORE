Pundi d'incontro

Photographic Research

A photographic exploration capturing places and states of mind where the veil between the tangible and the ethereal grows thin. This series juxtaposes natural, ethereal landscapes with human-centered, modern environments. Each shot invites viewers to navigate these liminal zones, transcending ordinary boundaries of perception to discover a space where visual dissonance resolves into harmony.
